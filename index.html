About Dave Keyz Premiere

Your front-row seat to the movies.

Our story

Dave Keyz Premiere started with a simple love of movies. Founded by Dave Keyz in Akure, Nigeria, it was built to be a place where movie lovers can find their next favorite film without scrolling through endless menus.Here you can watch the latest trailers, browse thousands of movies, save the ones you want to see, and share what you think in the comments. Everything is in one place, with a look inspired by the red carpet.We believe every movie night should begin with a great premiere. Whether you want action, comedy, romance, or a good scare, Dave Keyz Premiere is your front-row seat.

What we offer

  • Trailers for the newest and best movies, playing right on the page
  • Posters, ratings and details for thousands of films
  • Your own list of movies to watch later
  • Comments, so you can share what you thought

Our mission

Our mission is to help everyone find their next favorite movie. We bring the latest trailers, trending films, and timeless classics together in one easy place, so you spend less time searching and more time watching. We want Dave Keyz Premiere to be a home for movie lovers, where you can discover, save, and talk about the films you love.
